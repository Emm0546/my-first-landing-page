Our Story

The Seventh-day Adventist Church at Rue Des Jardins has been a lighthouse of truth, hope, and community service in Abidjan. We are fully committed to sharing the everlasting gospel and keeping the sacred Sabbath holy.

As Seventh-day Adventists, we are part of a worldwide Christian family that looks forward to the second coming of Jesus Christ and seeks to share the hope of the everlasting gospel with others.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make disciples of Jesus Christ who live as His loving witnesses and proclaim the everlasting gospel to all people in preparation for His soon return.

We seek to fulfill this mission through Christ-like living, teaching, serving, healing, and sharing the hope found in Jesus.

Our vision

We look forward to the restoration of all creation to harmony with God's perfect will and righteousness, and ultimately to the return of Jesus Christ.

Our Faith

The Bible is the foundation of our faith and guides our understanding of God, salvation, Christian living, and our mission in the world. Seventh-day Adventists hold 28 Fundamental Beliefs that express their understanding of biblical teaching.

We also observe the seventh-day Sabbath as a sacred day of rest, worship, fellowship, and service, following the biblical pattern of setting apart the seventh day as holy.

We'd Love to Welcome You

Whether you are discovering faith, looking for a church family, or simply seeking a place to worship, you are welcome at Rue Des Jardins.
