Our Ministries

At Rue Des Jardins, our ministries provide opportunities for people of all ages to grow in faith, serve others, build meaningful relationships, and participate in the mission of the church.

Sabbath School & Personal Ministries

We encourage Bible study, fellowship, personal spiritual growth, community outreach, and sharing the hope of the Gospel with others.

Adventist Youth (AY)

Our youth ministry helps young people develop a growing relationship with Jesus, discover their gifts, develop leadership skills, and serve their church and community.

Children's Ministries

We create engaging opportunities for children to learn about Jesus, grow in faith, build friendships, and develop a foundation for Christian living.

Women's Ministries

Women's Ministries encourages and supports women in their spiritual and personal development while helping them discover and use their gifts in the church, their families, and the wider community.

Family Ministries

Family Ministries seeks to strengthen relationships within families and equip individuals and families to build healthy, Christ-centered homes.

Music Ministries

Through music and worship, we use our gifts to glorify God, encourage the church family, and create an atmosphere where people can worship and draw closer to Christ.

Community Outreach

We seek to serve our neighbors through practical acts of kindness, compassion, and support while sharing the hope and love of Jesus Christ.

Find Your Place to Serve

There is a place for everyone to grow, serve, and make a difference. Come and discover how you can become part of our church family.
